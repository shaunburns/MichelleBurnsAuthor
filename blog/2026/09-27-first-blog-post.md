# The Beginning of the Journey

Every story has a beginning.

This one starts with an aspiration I've borne for a long time - to become an author and, one day, share a world of my imagination with readers who love fantasy novels as much as I do!

{/* truncate */}

If you've stumbled upon this corner of the internet, welcome! I'm incredibly grateful that you’ve taken the time to come on this adventure with me. 

Since the first words read by my parents, I've been fascinated by stories that launch us into worlds filled with magic, battles, ancient mysteries, thrilling journeys and characters who make us laugh, cry, groan, fist punch the air and *feel* deeply. 

For, in my opinion, the best books are the ones where you empathise with the characters. Whether they are the downtrodden hero, the villain who puts the ‘Q’ in questionable choices, or the side-kick that everyone loves to love, or hate. 

I've spent countless hours losing myself in these worlds as a reader.

Now, I'm attempting something rather different.

I'm creating one. 

# Psycharium

I'm currently 50,000 words into my first novel, Psycharium, which I’ve planned as the first book in a three-part epic fantasy series.

It's both exhilarating and slightly terrifying to share that.

The road ahead is long and fraught with wonder, peril and discovery. Chapters to be outlined and written, characters finding their voices, countless ideas to wrestle into something coherent, drafts to be edited again, and again, and again. During which, I expect, there’ll be more than a few moments where I'll wonder exactly what I've gotten myself into.

But the story is taking shape.

And for me, that feels like something worth sharing!

This website is a place where I can document that journey: my writing, the characters, the world-building, the challenges, the discoveries and, eventually, the road toward publication.

I'm not a published author. Yet.

But perhaps that's what makes this the perfect time to start.

Rather than appearing one day with a finished book and simply announcing it ( ... ta da!), I'd love for this website to be the place where the journey begins.

50,000 words that fill me with pride and inspire me to finish the story. 

If you're interested in fantasy stories, the process of writing a novel, or simply curious to see whether an aspiring author can turn an idea into a finished trilogy, please join me as we embark on this adventure together!

And hopefully, before long, Psycharium will leave my desk and find its way into your hands – and hearts. 

Until then, thank you for being here.

The journey has begun.
