# Psycharium

:::tip
Book one in a three-part epic fantasy series. Coming soon.
:::

In a kingdom where enhanced are imprisoned in psychariums, Alyssa Ruthford seeks to destroy the establishment that holds her captive.
But her prior efforts to escape and lay waste have been futile. Until now.

An unexpected catastrophe during the Reckoning trials compels Alyssa to escape the confinement of Calvere Psycharium - into a kingdom fraught with greater peril and intrigue than she is prepared for.
