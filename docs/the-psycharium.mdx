# The Psycharium

:::tip
The first book in the series. Coming soon.
:::

In a kingdom where enhanced beings are imprisoned in Psychariums, Alyssa Ruthford seeks to destroy the establishment that holds her captive.
But her prior efforts to escape and lay waste have been futile. Until now.

An unexpected catastrophe during the Reckoning trials compels Alyssa to escape the confinement of Calvere Phsycharium - into a kingdom fraught with greater peril and intrigue than she is prepared for.
